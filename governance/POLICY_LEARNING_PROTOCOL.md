# Policy Learning Protocol

## Flow

```text
Audit History
    ↓
Pattern Analysis
    ↓
Risk Detection
    ↓
Policy Proposal
    ↓
Review / Approval
    ↓
Policy Update
```

Rules:
- policies are proposed, not silently changed;
- audit evidence is retained;
- confidence scoring is used before adoption;
- approved policies improve future agent behavior.
